SWRV Birdsong is a recording artist, vocal coach, and multi-disciplinary creator who has spent over two decades learning how the voice works — not just mechanically, but as a full expression of identity.

The Curriculum

What You'll Master

🎙️

Breath & Support

Learn to use your diaphragm as your engine. Proper breath control is the foundation of power, range, and stamina in every performance.

🎼

Pitch & Tone

Develop your ear and your resonance. We work on pitch accuracy, tonal warmth, and the ability to color your sound intentionally across every register.

🔥

Runs & Agility

From basic scales to advanced melisma, you'll build the agility to move through notes cleanly and expressively — not just fast, but with meaning.

🧠

Performance Psychology

Technique without presence is empty. We address stage fear, vocal identity, authenticity, and how to hold a room with full conviction.

🎵

Genre Fluency

Gospel, R&B, pop, soul, hip-hop, worship — learn the stylistic differences and how to move between them without losing your core voice.

📻

Studio & Recording

Learn how your voice translates to tape. Mic technique, layering harmonies, and how to maximize your studio sessions like a professional.

Not sure where to start?
